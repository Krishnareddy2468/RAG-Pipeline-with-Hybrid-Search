Account Lockouts

Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts within 10 minutes. The lockout lasts 30 minutes. Support can manually unlock an account after verifying the user's identity.

Plan Upgrades

Customers can upgrade plans from the Billing page. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remaining billing period.

Plan Downgrades

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. Customers keep access to current plan features until the cycle ends.

Data Export

Workspace administrators can export account data from Settings > Compliance > Export Data. Large exports may take up to 24 hours to complete.

Support Priority

Enterprise customers receive priority support with a target first response time of 2 hours. Pro customers have a target first response time of 8 hours.
